Cost & Cash

The money. Where the numbers come from — and how companies go broke while profitable.

Track 1 taught you to read a project. This one teaches you to read its money. Where a unit rate actually comes from, why the BoQ and the schedule never quite line up, how physical percent is really measured — and the half nobody teaches a planner: cash. A contractor doesn't fail because of a bad CPI. It fails because it ran out of money while turning a profit.

Track 1 ends with a sentence that isn't true. In Week 18 we said actual cost was simple — just ask accounts. It isn't, and one day that costs somebody a great deal of money. Track 2 opens by going back for it.

THE ROADMAP

Tracks 3 and 4

Cost and schedule are only half the discipline. Risk explains why projects go wrong before they do; claims decide who pays when they have. These are the next two tracks in the path — and the two that pay the most.

Track 3 · Risk Management

Where most content stops — and where real delays begin. Built on PMI's Risk practice: identification, qualitative & quantitative analysis, Monte Carlo, contingency, response, monitoring, opportunities vs threats. Not "filling a risk register" — a system that creates value.

On the roadmap

Track 4 · Claims & Delay Analysis

When things go wrong, who pays? Forensic delay analysis, extension of time, disruption, productivity loss, evidence, and how daily reports become the foundation of a defensible claim.

THE TOOLBOX

The Toolbox

The tools. Take these in any order, whenever you need them.

These aren't a track — they're a shelf. The learning path above teaches you what to think; this is where you learn to build it. Nothing here is a prerequisite for anything else, so start with whatever your Monday morning demands.

Strategy before software. The fundamentals stay free, and they come first — a tool you can drive but not reason about is worth nothing on a site. Once the logic is yours, these are the tools that put it to work.
