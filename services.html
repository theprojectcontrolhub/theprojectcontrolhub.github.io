Services

Direct help, beyond the curriculum.

The curriculum stays free. For teams and engineers who want hands-on help with their own project, there are two areas of work: project controls and training, and contracts, claims and tender advisory.

Project Controls & Training

  • Project planning & scheduling

    Schedules that hold up: logic, baselines and updates that match how the job is actually built.

    • Schedule development and review
    • Baseline set-up and updates
    • Primavera P6 structure, coding and layouts

    Typical deliverables, depending on scope: a reviewed or rebuilt programme, a baseline report and an update procedure.

  • Project controls

    Making the schedule, the cost report and the risk register tell management the same story.

    • Progress and earned value measurement
    • Cost and risk reporting
    • Controls health checks on live projects

    Typical deliverables, depending on scope: a monthly controls report, progress and earned value dashboards, or a health-check note.

  • Training

    Practical training on the tools project controls runs on, built on the same eight tracks as this site.

    • Excel and Power BI for project controls
    • Primavera P6 and reporting tools
    • Workshops and one-to-one mentoring

    Typical deliverables, depending on scope: session material and worked Excel or Power BI files your team keeps.

Contracts, Claims & Tender Advisory

  • Claims, EOT & delay analysis

    Building and testing entitlement to time and money, from the first notice to the delay analysis behind it.

    • Claims management
    • Extension of time (EOT)
    • Delay analysis

    Typical deliverables, depending on scope: a delay-event chronology, an EOT submission or review, and a delay analysis report.

    Available on request; the scope is agreed for each enquiry.

    The Claims & Delay Analysis track

  • Contract management & FIDIC

    Day-to-day contract administration and FIDIC advice, on the Employer’s side or the contractor’s.

    • Contract management and administration
    • FIDIC contract advisory (Red and Yellow Book)
    • Interface management across contracts

    Typical deliverables, depending on scope: contract and notice registers, advice notes on clauses, and a contract status report.

  • Tender preparation

    Turning design into a priced, procurable package for EPC work, from feasibility through to detailed design.

    • BOQ and material take-off
    • Bid packages for EPC procurement
    • Technical deliverables for tender

    Typical deliverables, depending on scope: BOQ and material take-off, bid package documents and technical tender deliverables.
