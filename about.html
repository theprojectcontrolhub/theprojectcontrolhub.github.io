Why I built this

Most of what's written about project controls is either too theoretical to be useful or too tool-specific to transfer. You read about Primavera P6 without understanding what a schedule is actually for. You learn EVM formulas without knowing when and why they matter on a real project.

I built this site to bridge that gap. Everything here starts from first principles — why things work the way they do — and then moves into the practical tools and techniques that make a difference on site.

This isn't a course catalogue or a community platform. It's a structured series of articles and videos, published weekly, built around the way I wish I had been taught when I was starting out. If you're a planning engineer, a project controls specialist, or simply someone trying to understand how large construction projects are actually managed — this is for you.
