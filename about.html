ABOUT

Learning Project Controls Through Real Projects

I'm Barış Emre Özçelik — a Planning & Project Controls Lead, Civil Engineer, and PMP®. I've spent the last eight years delivering project controls on some of the largest construction projects in the world.

Throughout my career, I've learned one thing again and again: successful projects are not driven by software. They're driven by better planning, sound decisions, and effective project controls.

That's exactly why I created The Project Control Hub.

Why this hub exists

Most Project Controls resources focus on teaching software — how to use Primavera P6, how to create Gantt charts, how to build reports.

Those skills matter. But they're only part of the picture.

This platform focuses on the principles behind successful projects: planning, scheduling, risk, forecasting, progress measurement, and decision-making. Every article is based on lessons learned from real construction projects — not classroom theory.

My philosophy

Planning is not about creating schedules.

Planning is about reducing uncertainty — and helping teams make better decisions.

That philosophy is the foundation of every article, diagram, and lesson published here.

Let's learn together

The Project Control Hub is an ongoing learning series for engineers, planners, and project professionals who want to develop practical Project Controls skills.

New lessons are published every week, each building on the last to create a structured learning journey.

If you're ready to think beyond software and understand how real projects are actually controlled — welcome.
